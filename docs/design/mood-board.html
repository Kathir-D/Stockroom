F2Inter + mono for identifiers
Canon 70-200mm f/2.8
Lenses › Zooms › Canon 70-200mm f/2.8
SerialC70200-001 CustodianAlex Rivera · 200481 Checked out2026-09-05 14:32 Due2026-09-12

Anything printed on a barcode sticker renders in mono. Makes serials and student numbers unmistakably machine data, and 0/O and 1/l stop being ambiguous when someone reads one aloud. Costs a second font file.

Deliberately absent from every option: purple/indigo gradients, glassmorphism, glow effects, neon accents on dark, oversized border-radius, animated blobs, and colour used decoratively. Hue in this app means state and nothing else. That constraint is what makes it read as a tool rather than a template.
