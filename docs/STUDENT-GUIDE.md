# Borrowing equipment with Stockroom

Print this page and tape it above the checkout computer.

## Taking something out

1. **Scan your ID card.** You are signed in. No password needed.
2. **Find what you need.** Pick a type on the left (Cameras, Lenses, Audio...), or type in the search box.
3. **Click the item, then Add to cart.** Nothing is yours yet. Add everything you need for the shoot.
4. **Open the cart and pick a due date.** Seven days at most.
5. **Press Check out.** Everything in the cart is now signed out to you.
6. **Sign out** when it asks. Other people use this computer.

Kits (a camera, lens and bag that go together) are under **Kits** in the sidebar. A kit only goes out whole. If one piece is missing, ask the teacher.

## Bringing it back

**Scan the sticker on the item.** That's it. It is checked in the moment the scanner beeps, whoever you are and whoever borrowed it.

If something is broken or a piece is missing, press **Add a note** on the screen that appears and say what happened. A broken camera that nobody mentions stays broken for the next person.

## The first time you scan your card

It asks you to choose a password. Pick one you will remember, 8 characters or more. After that, scanning your card still signs you in with no password. You only need the password if you type your number by hand.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Return your overdue item first" | Something you borrowed is late. Bring it back and scan it in, then you can borrow again. |
| "Scan your student ID to sign in first" | You scanned an item before signing in. Scan your ID card, then the item. |
| Your card does not scan | Type your number and your password. No password yet? Ask the teacher to set one. |
| An item's sticker is missing or will not scan | Give it to the teacher. Do not leave it on the shelf. |
| Somebody else has the thing you want | The item shows who has it and when it is due back. |

You are signed out automatically after 10 minutes of doing nothing, and your cart is cleared.
